- Pennsylvania stores on Chuck E. Cheese's participating list include Altoona, Erie, Northeast Philadelphia, Snyder Plaza (Philadelphia), Springfield and Wilkes-Barre Township. Whitehall and Warrington also have Sensory Sensitive Sunday pages.
- Participation can change, so call your local store before you go.
- New in 2026: Chuck E. Cheese also offers Sensory Sensitive Birthdays, booked through its website.
