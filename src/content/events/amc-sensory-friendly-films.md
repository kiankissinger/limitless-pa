---
title: AMC Sensory Friendly Films
host: AMC Theatres
recurring: "Second and fourth Saturday of every month (family-friendly films), plus Wednesday evenings (films for mature audiences), at participating theatres"
location: Participating AMC theatres
region: Statewide
summary: "AMC turns the lights up and the sound down, and kids are welcome to get up, move around, dance or make noise. Shows start right on time with no trailers."
accommodations:
  - Lights up, sound down
  - Free to move around
url: https://www.amctheatres.com/programs/sensory-friendly-films
---

- AMC runs this program with the Autism Society. Not every theatre takes part, so search "Sensory Friendly" on the AMC website or app to find your local showtimes.
- Arrive a few minutes early, because the movie starts at the listed time with no previews.
- In the Philadelphia area, AMC Marple 10 in Springfield lists Sensory Friendly Films among its amenities.
