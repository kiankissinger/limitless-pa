- On Sensory-Friendly Days the museum opens early at 9 a.m., and local groups that support autistic kids and their families take part.
- The December 2025 Sensory-Friendly Day had two sensory-friendly showings of *The Sky Tonight* and a quiet cool-down space set apart from the exhibits.
- The museum's accessibility page lists sensory backpacks and other help available any day.
