- The Quiet Room is beneath the Lost Kennywood main entrance and opens with a four-digit code. You get the code when you enroll in the Alternative Access Program, or at the Service Center by Noah's Ark.
- The park recommends getting an IBCCES Accessibility Card ahead of time, but you still enroll at the park, and accommodations aren't guaranteed.
- Pick up the Accessibility Guide online or at the Service Center. Heads-up: some coasters use quick flashing lights, which stand out more at night.
