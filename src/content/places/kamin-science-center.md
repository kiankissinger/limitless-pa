- During sensory events there's a quiet "take a break" room with fidget toys, beanbag chairs, calm music and low lighting.
- Sensory backpacks and headphones can be borrowed during regular hours, not just on sensory days.
- The center has also held Sensory Sensitive 18+ Nights, a calm, kid-free evening for young adults and grown-ups. Watch its events page.
