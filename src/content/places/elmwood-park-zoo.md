- Ask at admissions for a free sensory pack. Packs hold noise-cancelling headphones, fidget toys, a stress ball, thinking putty, and the zoo's social story, sensory map and sensory guide.
- The zoo's pages don't agree on where the quiet space is (a gated area with seats and sensory activity boards), so ask at admissions when you arrive.
- Quiet Mornings let a small number of guests in before the zoo opens. See the Events page for details.
