- The sensory room is on the West Lower Suite Level. Take the elevators behind sections 105/205.
- Sensory bags include noise-cancelling headphones, fidget tools, verbal cue cards and weighted lap pads. You'll find Guest Services booths behind sections 121, 136, 206 and 226.
- The stadium suggests the free KultureCity app for previewing sensory features before game day. The phone number above is the Guest Services hotline for accessibility needs.
