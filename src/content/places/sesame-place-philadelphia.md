- Look over the park's 2026 Sensory Guide before you go. It rates each ride and show on a 1-to-10 sensory scale.
- Older guides and newer announcements put the quiet room in different spots. Ask at the Welcome Center where it is now and for the code to get in.
- Noise-cancelling headphones are lent out first come, first served. The park also lists low-sensory spots for parade viewing and dining.
- Ask about the Ride Accessibility Program if waiting in line is hard for your child.
