- Sensory bags are at the Membership Booth and Mobility Rentals. They hold noise-reducing headphones, sunglasses, fidget toys and non-verbal cue cards. You'll sign one out and leave an ID as a deposit.
- Look over the Sensory Map on the zoo's website before your visit, and watch for quiet-zone and headphone-zone signs on the grounds.
- The zoo has held Sensory Friendly Mornings in the past and has a page of tips for them. Check its events page for upcoming dates.
